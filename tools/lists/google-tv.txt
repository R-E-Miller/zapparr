# Google TV: every box or TV whose home screen is com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx (Chromecast
# with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, onn, Xiaomi TV Box S, and Sony, TCL, Hisense or Philips sets
# with Google TV).
#
# platform: google-tv
#
# `debloat.py plan` (with no list named) uses this list on every Google TV device, together with any
# vendor or model list in this folder whose "match:" line fits the box; `debloat.py probe` names them.
# One package per line; everything after # is a comment. Packages that aren't installed are skipped,
# and nothing is uninstalled: `python tools/debloat.py restore` re-enables all of it.
#
# Only packages that tested lists on many Google TV devices disable with nothing reported broken.
# Sources, cited next to each group:
#   [CYT n/129]  CleanYourTV's tested per-device lists (examples/<model>/debloat.txt, MIT license,
#                (c) 2026 Gathin), checked 2026-10-30: n of its 129 Google TV lists disable it.
#                https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv
#   [RCL]        rcland12/debloat-tv, packages-chromecast.txt, tested on a Chromecast with Google TV 4K
#                (Android 14): https://github.com/rcland12/debloat-tv
#
# Not here on purpose:
#   - The stock launcher and the setup wizard: `debloat.py set-home` handles them, once Zapparr is Home.
#   - Anything debloat.py protects (Play Store and Services, TV Settings, keyboards, TvProvider, the
#     remote, Bluetooth and network services), and anything the box opens Settings or its inputs with,
#     or that created a saved Wi-Fi network: plan skips those whatever a list says.
#   - com.google.android.tvrecommendations: protected, see debloat.py.
#   - YouTube, YouTube Music, Netflix, Prime Video and other apps: yours to keep or switch off.
#
# Kept by default, but yours to decide. Remove the leading "# " to disable one:
# com.google.android.katniss                    # Assistant and voice search, also Zapparr's Search mic [CYT 33/129]
# com.google.android.apps.mediashell            # Chromecast built-in: casting from your phone [CYT 18/129]
# com.google.android.apps.tv.dreamx             # ambient-mode screensaver; also holds the Google Home panel (lights) [CYT 52/129]
